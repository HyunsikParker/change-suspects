# Change Suspects privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Change Suspects is a Forge app that runs on Atlassian's platform inside your Jira site.

## What the app reads
As the person using it and with that person's permissions: the work item they open the panel on, work items resolved
or recorded as deployed in the days around that work item's start (summary, description, labels, components, status,
dates and the app's issue property), the site's issue link types and field list, and the person's time zone setting.

## What the app writes
Only when a person asks for it: the start time of an incident and the changes marked as its suspected cause (stored as
an issue property named change-suspects on that work item), an issue link between an incident and a marked change, a
timeline comment on the incident, and fictional sample work items (Load sample incidents). Everything it writes stays
in your Atlassian site.

## What the app stores
Nothing outside your Jira site. The app has no database of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian. The developer
does not collect, receive or sell personal data. Atlassian's platform keeps its usual operational logs for Forge apps;
see Atlassian's privacy policy.

## Contact
Questions and requests: the issues page of the app's public code repository.
